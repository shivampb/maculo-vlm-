      Before you start

      • This is not a diagnosis. Maculo ranks how closely a photo matches the conditions on your list. It cannot tell you what you have.
      • It only knows your list. If the right condition isn't listed, the top match will still be wrong.
      • See a professional about any spot that changes, bleeds, itches or worries you.
      • Your photos aren't stored. They're kept in memory for up to 15 minutes, then forgotten.

      How Maculo works

      1. Outline. When you click, the Segment Anything model (SAM) draws the outline of the spot under your cursor.
      2. Compare. A vision-language model trained on skin images scores how well the outlined area matches a description of each condition on your list.
      3. Rank. The scores are turned into percentages that add up to 100% across your list. A high score means “closest of these”, not “likely”.

      Results change with the photo's lighting, focus and framing, and with the list you choose. Treat them as a starting point for questions, never as an answer.
