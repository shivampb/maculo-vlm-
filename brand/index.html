Color

Forest and lime lead.

  • Deep Forest for text, buttons and dark surfaces.
  • Lime for highlights and big moments, always with Forest or black text. Never lime text on white (1.4:1).
  • Sky for the point in the mark, info notes and charts.
  • Green #1E7A3C for links and green text on white.
  • Interfaces stay mostly white. Roughly 70% white, 20% Forest, 10% lime and sky.

Type

One family: Outfit.

  • SemiBold 600 for titles and the wordmark, tracking −0.03em.
  • Regular 400 for body text, Light 300 for large intros.
  • Sentence case. Tabular numbers for scores.
  • Free under the SIL Open Font License. Lufga can replace it if licensed.
